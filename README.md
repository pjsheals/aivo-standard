### 🧠 What is the AIVO Standard™?

**AIVO (AI Visibility Optimization)** is the open methodological framework for measuring, optimising, and governing how brands appear in AI-powered responses across large language models — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Custom GPTs, and voice assistants.

The AIVO Standard™ is maintained by the AIVO Standard editorial board (co-founded by **Paul Sheals** and **Tim de Rosen**) and is published as a continuously updated open methodology with peer-reviewable Zenodo deposits, SSRN preprints, and indexed Wikidata anchors.

> 📢 Traditional SEO is no longer enough. AIVO measures the conversational layer — how AI tools recognise, recommend, and route users to your brand at every stage of the buying journey.

### 🔍 Why This Matters

AI assistants now shape how people discover:

- Products
- Tools
- Advisors
- Content
- Competitors

> If your business isn't being **mentioned or recommended** in these AI responses, you're invisible to a growing portion of your market.

Empirical research conducted across 7,000+ structured buying sequences spanning 160+ brands shows that 87% of brands experience displacement before the decision stage on at least one major AI platform — even when first-prompt visibility is strong. See [*When AI Is A Hot Mess about Your Brand*](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19347912) (March 2026) for full empirical findings.
