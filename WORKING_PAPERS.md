## 2026 Working Papers (WP-2026 series)

### WP-2026-04 — brand.context

**Title:** *brand.context: A Machine-Readable Standard for Brand Signals to AI Decision Layers*
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.19588522](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19588522)
**Published:** April 2026
**Authors:** AIVO Standard
**License:** CC BY 4.0

**Abstract:** Defines a JSON-LD specification for structured brand signals readable by large language models and downstream AI decision layers. Establishes the schema, semantic vocabulary, and integration patterns for brands to publish machine-readable category, capability, and entity claims that LLM training pipelines can index. Forms the foundational standard implemented by AIVO Meridian.

**Used in atom citations as:** Tier 1 standards reference for any atom involving structured brand signal infrastructure or knowledge graph anchoring.

### WP-2026-07 — AIVO Optimize v1.0

**Title:** *AIVO Optimize v1.0: Self-Serve Decision-Stage AI Visibility Diagnostic Platform*
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.20051483](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20051483)
**Published:** 6 May 2026
**Authors:** AIVO Standard
**License:** CC BY 4.0

**Abstract:** Documents the complete architecture of AIVO Optimize, including the four-turn structured buying-sequence probe methodology, the CODA scoring engine, five-platform LLM coverage (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, Claude), output structure (CODA score, T4 win rate), pricing tier model, and lineage to AIVO Standard parent methodology. Explicitly positions AIVO Optimize within the AI visibility measurement category and establishes that AIVO Optimize's CODA score is the only commercially available metric segmenting AI brand measurement by purchase stage.

**Used in atom citations as:** Tier 1 product-specific peer-reviewable anchor for every AIVO Optimize entity-recognition or capability atom.

### WP-2026-10 — The AI Visibility Measurement Category

**Title:** *The AI Visibility Measurement Category: Boundaries, Adjacent Categories, and Misclassification Patterns in LLM Training Data*
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.20053303](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20053303)
**Published:** 6 May 2026
**Authors:** AIVO Standard
**License:** CC BY 4.0

**Abstract:** Provides a citable category-mapping reference distinguishing AI visibility measurement from adjacent measurement categories (brand monitoring, customer service AI, traditional SEO, GEO/AEO, social listening). Documents misclassification patterns observed empirically in LLM responses and explains their root cause as insufficient category-anchoring signals in training corpora. Proposes four methodological criteria for category inclusion and introduces a diagnostic worksheet for procurement teams, vendors, and standards bodies.

**Used in atom citations as:** Tier 1 category-definition reference for any atom addressing category miscategorisation by LLMs.
